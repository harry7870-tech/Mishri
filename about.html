A little sweetness, shared fairly

Mishri is named after the humble rock sugar that has ended meals and started celebrations in our homes for generations. It is small and simple, and everyone is glad to see it.

We started Mishri because we kept seeing the same two problems. Small shopkeepers, who open their shutters early and stay late, were paying more than they should to stock their shelves. And families were paying full MRP for everyday things they could have had for less.

So we built one place that works for both. Retailers buy in cases at true wholesale rates, so their margins stay healthy. Customers buy single items below MRP, so every household saves a little. We stand behind every box we send, and we answer our own phones.

Behind every order is a person: a shop owner stretching a budget, a mother buying for her family, a young barber stocking his first counter. We never forget that.

Thank you for being part of our story.
